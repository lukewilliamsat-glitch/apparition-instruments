Each volume input connects through its capacitor to its tone wiper; grounded track ends and all four cases share ground. Pickups, selector, jack and external connections intentionally omitted.

## Scope and acceptance

Six persistent component groups, four pots and two capacitors. A first rendered comparison identified an insufficient terminal flange and an excessively flat capacitor face; both were corrected. Final geometry includes the projecting phenolic terminal flange, pear-shaped eyelets and flat bent terminal stems, round-section K-case shoulders and curved dot-matrix printing. Soft contact shadows reveal construction depth; their map refreshes on assembly movement, reduced-motion scrolling and responsive resize. Case solder fillets are seated on the tin surface; shared common-ground joints are represented once. Final framing accommodates the wider photo-derived layout without changing the page structure. Lead endpoints derived from the same transformed terminal geometry, smooth cloth/conductor paths, stripped ends, insulation and solder. Existing forward/reverse progression and Signal Forge calculations retained. Treble bleed response remains selectable, independently of physical hardware. No new dependencies or paid media.

Technical and rendered acceptance, exact sealed artifact and publication receipt follow below. Owner aesthetic response remains separate.
