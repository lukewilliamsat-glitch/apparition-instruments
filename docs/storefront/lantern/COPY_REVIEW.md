## Complete FAQ answer

> You can build and review a basket of supported components and wiring kits. Your basket is saved in this browser; it is not a confirmed order or a stock reservation. When ordering is available, checkout checks current prices, stock and delivery charges before an authorised secure payment handoff. Checkout may be temporarily unavailable. Returning from payment does not, by itself, confirm a paid order: payment must be independently confirmed.

## Startup failure wording

Route heading/status identifies Components, product, basket or checkout as temporarily unavailable, followed by:

> We couldn’t check the current product information. Please try again later. Your basket is not a confirmed order or a stock reservation.

> If you have saved a basket in this browser, this page has not changed it. Ordering stays unavailable until current prices, stock and checkout can be checked.

Actions: **Reload this page**, **Ordering help**, **Contact us**. No internal status number, exception message, stack or configuration is interpolated.
